# Project IRIS M

**Vision · Health · Personal State**

### Project Overview

IRIS M is a future smart eyewear concept within the M-PIN ecosystem.

Its philosophy is simple:

**Make glasses smarter at what glasses are meant to do.**

Rather than adding cameras, displays, or conversational AI, IRIS M explores how existing technologies can improve vision, support personal health monitoring, and preserve user-controlled digital continuity.

### 1. Vision First

IRIS M explores adaptive optical technologies that could provide:

- Adjustable prescription settings
- Near- and far-distance focus adjustment
- Automatic or manual focus control
- Optional photochromic lenses for sun protection

The long-term goal is to offer an alternative to conventional progressive lenses while maintaining the appearance of ordinary premium eyewear.

### 2. Classic Eyewear Design

IRIS M prioritizes the appearance and comfort of traditional glasses.

Its design concept includes:

- Premium black acetate frames
- Subtle gold accents
- Slim temples
- Softly curved temple tips that wrap naturally behind the ears
- Balanced weight distribution
- Adaptable fitting for different facial structures

The objective is to integrate technology without making the glasses look like an electronic device.

### 3. Health Monitoring

The area behind the ear offers opportunities for wearable health sensing.

IRIS M explores existing technologies for:

- Skin temperature monitoring
- Motion and activity sensing
- Heart-rate monitoring where reliable sensor contact is achievable
- Limited EEG-related signal acquisition using skin-contact electrodes

The inner surface of the curved temple tip is a potential location for discreet biometric electrodes.

These capabilities remain research concepts. Medical accuracy, safety, and clinical validity would require independent verification.

### 4. Detachable M-PIN Module

A central concept of IRIS M is its detachable personal-state module.

The M-PIN module is designed to attach to the lower inner portion of the temple tip, beneath and behind the ear.

Its intended characteristics include:

- Minimal size and weight
- Concealed placement during normal wear
- Easy attachment and removal
- Owner-controlled personal settings
- Portability between compatible eyewear devices

The module may carry authorized vision settings, personal preferences, and selected health-related state.

**The glasses may change. The Owner's personal state remains with the Owner.**

### 5. Reusable Eyewear

IRIS M explores a future in which eyewear hardware and personal settings are independent.

A second-hand buyer could attach their own M-PIN module and configure a compatible pair of glasses using their personal settings.

This would remain subject to the supported optical adjustment range, prescription requirements, secure device reset, and professional fitting where necessary.

### 6. Privacy by Design

IRIS M does not require:

- Outward-facing cameras
- Visual displays
- Conversational AI
- Continuous storage of personal history by the eyewear manufacturer

The design emphasizes useful functionality, personal privacy, and Owner-controlled information.

### 7. Long-Term Vision

IRIS M begins with the fundamental purpose of eyewear: helping people see.

Its longer-term vision includes better visual assistance, accessible health monitoring, and potential integration with future vision-restoration technologies.

These are future research directions, not claims of existing product capabilities.

### Project Status

**Concept Exploration — Not a Commercial Product**

IRIS M is a long-term M-PIN ecosystem concept.

No manufacturing commitment, completed integrated prototype, medical certification, or commercial availability is claimed.

The project explores how existing technologies could eventually be combined into practical, human-centered eyewear.
