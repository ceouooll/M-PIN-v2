# Project AURORA — Public Scope

Project AURORA is intentionally developed with a public concept layer and a non-public implementation layer.

The goal is to make the project understandable, inspectable, and historically traceable without exposing unnecessary manufacturing, security, or supplier-sensitive information.

---

## Public

The following may be openly documented:

- Project purpose
- User experience goals
- High-level feature concepts
- Concept renders
- Public design direction
- Incoming call awareness
- Phone separation awareness
- Emerald light interaction concept
- High-level wearable architecture
- Relationship to M-PIN
- Prototype status
- Public development history
- High-level manufacturing philosophy
- Public testing results

---

## Public with Abstraction

The following may be discussed only at a high level:

- Universal core concept
- Size-adjustment concept
- Outer-shell modularity
- RF window concept
- Charging concept
- Waterproofing strategy
- Assembly sequence
- Battery strategy
- Haptic strategy
- Antenna placement principles

Exact production details are intentionally omitted.

---

## Intentionally Non-Public

The following may remain private during development:

- Exact mechanical dimensions and tolerances
- PCB layout
- Battery part numbers and suppliers
- Antenna geometry
- RF tuning parameters
- Waterproofing compounds and sealing recipes
- Size-insert locking geometry
- Adhesive specifications
- Assembly-jig design
- Manufacturing drawings
- OEM / ODM partner identities
- Supplier pricing
- MOQ negotiations
- Production contracts
- Internal test limits
- Security-sensitive implementation details
- Private keys, credentials, and infrastructure information

---

## Visual Material

Public images may include:

- Concept renders
- Non-production prototypes
- Public reference diagrams
- High-level exploded views
- User experience illustrations

Concept images must be clearly identified as conceptual when they do not represent a production device.

---

## Principle

**Open the idea, the purpose, the evidence, and the public development history.**

**Protect the details that materially affect security, manufacturing advantage, supplier relationships, or production reliability.**

---

## Status

This public-scope policy applies to Project AURORA while it remains in concept and prototype development.

The boundary may evolve as the project progresses.
